Who is responsible

HUEO is an independent photo editor for iOS developed by Ariel Marques Aguiar, based in São Paulo, Brazil. Ariel is responsible for the personal information processed for HUEO. This policy covers the app, its editing extension in Apple Photos, the HUEO pages on ariel.works, and the beta signup form.

For privacy questions or requests, email ariel.marques77@gmail.com.

Your photos and edits

Photo editing and rendering take place on your device. HUEO stores imported photos, previews, and editing adjustments in its local app storage. The Photos extension receives the photo you choose to edit and returns the edited result to Apple Photos.

You decide which photos to import, save to Photos, or share with another app. Those destinations handle the files under their own privacy practices. Device backups and iCloud Photos follow your Apple settings.

HUEO’s usage events describe interactions with its tools and Looks. Their custom parameters exclude photo pixels, thumbnails, filenames, photo-library asset identifiers, and photo metadata such as EXIF or GPS coordinates.

Usage analytics

HUEO uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features people use and to guide product improvements. Events can describe opening the editor, importing and saving, exporting, using editing tools, applying Looks, managing Favorites, and using the editing guide. Some tool values are recorded as broad ranges.

Firebase also collects technical information, including app and iOS versions, device model, language, session activity, and identifiers for the app installation and, depending on the SDK configuration, the device. Analytics derives approximate geographic information from IP addresses. These identifiers let events from the same installation be associated over time.

Control usage sharing

In HUEO builds that include Privacy settings, usage sharing is enabled by default. Open the library menu → Privacy → Share usage data to turn it off or on. Turning it off stops future Analytics collection and clears pending Analytics data on the device. Information already sent is handled according to the retention and deletion practices below.

Earlier TestFlight builds may collect usage data without this in-app switch. Update to a build with Privacy settings to manage the preference. You can also contact Ariel about your information.

Crash reports and feedback

Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports to help diagnose problems and improve stability. Reports can include stack traces, app and device details, installation identifiers, and technical context such as which feature failed. Firebase’s session components also process technical session and app-quality information. When Analytics is enabled, Crashlytics may receive recent Analytics events as diagnostic breadcrumbs.

Crash reporting operates automatically. The Share usage data switch controls Firebase Analytics. Crashlytics has its own collection behavior and continues to report diagnostics when that switch is off.

If you use Send Feedback, HUEO prepares a message with your comments, app version and build, iOS version, device category, and locale. You review and send it through Mail or a sharing destination you choose. The message template contains technical information and text fields for your feedback. Any content you add is included in your message.

Beta signup

If you join the beta through the website, the form collects your first name, email address, iPhone model, user profile, photo-editing frequency, and agreement to receive beta emails. The record also includes its source, signup date, and waitlist status. Supabase stores these details so Ariel can manage invitations, communicate about the beta, and understand the testing group.

Signing up is voluntary. Your agreement covers HUEO beta and TestFlight communications. You can ask Ariel to remove your signup or stop these emails at any time.

Apple handles TestFlight distribution and provides developers with beta testing information such as installation and session activity, crashes, and feedback under TestFlight & Privacy.

Service providers

Google Firebase processes usage and diagnostic information. Supabase stores beta signup records. Apple provides Photos, app distribution, and TestFlight. The email or sharing provider you choose handles feedback messages, and the website’s hosting and font providers deliver these pages.

These services may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, according to their infrastructure, terms, and applicable data-transfer safeguards. Access to HUEO’s service accounts is used to operate the app, diagnose problems, and manage the beta. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

Read more in Firebase Privacy and Security, Google’s Privacy Policy, Supabase’s Privacy Policy, and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

  • Local photos and edits remain in HUEO until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Copies exported to Photos, other apps, or backups follow the storage and deletion controls of those destinations.
  • Analytics user and event records follow the retention settings of HUEO’s Google Analytics property. Aggregated reports can remain available longer. Contact Ariel for the current retention period and requests relating to your records. See Google’s retention documentation.
  • Google states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning their removal from live and backup systems. See Firebase’s retention details.
  • Beta signup and support records are kept while needed to manage the beta, answer requests, and follow up on reported issues. Ariel reviews deletion requests and may retain information required for legal obligations or resolving disputes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may request access, correction, deletion, information about sharing, portability, restriction, or objection to the processing of your personal information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. You may also contact your data-protection authority, including Brazil’s ANPD.

Send requests to ariel.marques77@gmail.com, describing the service or record concerned. Ariel may ask for the information needed to verify the request and locate the relevant record. Installation-based Analytics and diagnostic records can be difficult to associate with an email address, so Ariel will explain the available options and any limits.

You can manage Analytics sharing in compatible HUEO builds, control Photos access in iOS settings, and request removal from the beta list. The app’s editing features remain available with Analytics sharing switched off.

This website

Your browser stores your EN or PT-BR language choice locally. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Hosting and font providers receive technical request information, such as IP address and browser headers, to deliver the pages and font files. Beta form submissions are sent to Supabase when you submit the form.

Policy updates

This page will be updated as HUEO’s features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app, this website, or beta email where appropriate.
